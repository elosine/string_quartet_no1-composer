String Quartet No. 1

Justin Wen-Lo Yang · reading your part from the screen

Opening your part

  • Open your link in Safari on an iPad or tablet, held sideways. A laptop works too.
  • Wait for the bar to fill, then press Open. The film of your part is now on your device.
  • Please use Safari itself, not an icon added to the Home Screen.
  • If the tablet's battery icon is yellow (Low Power Mode), switch that mode off.

Reading it

  • Your part is six rows. Each row is eight seconds, left to right. The numbers along the top of a row are seconds.
  • The coloured line is the cursor. Play what it reaches, when it reaches it.
  • The rows behind the cursor are replaced by the music to come, so you can always read ahead.

Playing by yourself

  • Play alone moves only your screen. Press it again to stop.
  • Touch the score to put the cursor on that spot. This works while you are stopped.
  • The two arrows move back or forward a row.
  • The number is the time in seconds. Touch it, type a number, press Go.
  • The 100% button slows the piece: 85, 70 or 50 percent.

Playing together

  • Anyone can start the group. Go to the spot, then press Play all.
  • Every screen jumps to one second before that spot and starts together. The frame turns orange and names who started.
  • Stop all, from any screen, stops everyone on the same place.
  • While the group is playing, your own controls rest.

If something looks wrong

  • If a Tap to play button appears, tap it.
  • If your screen falls behind or freezes, reload the page and press Open. It rejoins the group where the group is.
  • "not connected" at the foot of the controls means your device has lost the network. Your part still plays alone.

The performance instructions for the notation are in the score.
