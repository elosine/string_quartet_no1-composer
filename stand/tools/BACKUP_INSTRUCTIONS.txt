THE BACKUP STAND - String Quartet No. 1
=======================================

What it is
  The same stand as the website, running on this laptop.
  Use it if the website cannot be reached at the venue.
  The players' tablets join this laptop over Wi-Fi, so no internet is needed.


ONCE, BEFORE THE DAY (at home)
------------------------------
1. Node must be installed on this laptop.
   If START_BACKUP.bat says it is not, install the LTS version from https://nodejs.org

2. Copy this whole folder onto the laptop, for example to C:\sq1-backup
   (Unzip sq1-backup.zip there, if it came as a zip.)

3. Rehearse it at home, exactly as on the day (below), with your phone's hotspot
   and one tablet. This is the real test: it proves the phone lets the tablet
   reach the laptop.

4. The first time it runs, Windows may ask whether Node may use the network.
   Tick BOTH boxes (Private and Public networks), then Allow.


ON THE DAY, IF THE WEBSITE FAILS
--------------------------------
1. Turn on your phone's hotspot.

2. Join the laptop AND every tablet to that hotspot.

3. On the laptop, double-click START_BACKUP.bat
   A black window opens. Keep it open the whole time.
   The browser opens the LINK SHEET: one QR code per part.

4. Each player scans their part's QR code with the tablet's camera, opens it
   in Safari, waits for the bar, presses Open.
   The film loads from the laptop, about 50 MB each, so allow a minute.

5. Everything works as before: Play all, Stop all, Play alone.
   The piece starts at the beginning.

To stop: close the black window.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Tablets: set Auto-Lock to Never for the reading
  (Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock).
  The backup cannot keep the screens awake by itself.

- Keep the laptop plugged in, and stop it from sleeping.

- The links the players were emailed point at the website. They do NOT reach
  the laptop. Use the QR codes on the link sheet.

- If the sheet says the laptop is on no network: join the hotspot, close the
  black window, double-click START_BACKUP.bat again.

- The link sheet holds every player's link. Show it on the laptop screen only.
