String Quartet No. 1

Justin Yang

Playing together

  • Anyone can start the group. Press Play all to start the score.
  • While the group is playing, controls become inactive until stop.

Playing solo

  • Play alone moves the score only on your screen.
  • To move the cursor: while the score is stopped, tap anywhere to move the cursor to that spot.
  • The two arrows move back or forward a row.
  • The number is the time in seconds. Touch it, type a number, press Go to jump to that time in the score.
  • Toggle the 100% button to change the play speed of the score.

If something looks wrong

  • If a Tap to play button appears, tap it.
  • If your screen falls behind or freezes, reload the page and press Open. It rejoins the group where the group is.
  • "not connected" at the foot of the controls means your device has lost the network. Your part still plays alone.

Notes

  • Please use Safari itself, not an icon added to the Home Screen.
  • If the tablet's battery icon is yellow (Low Power Mode), switch that mode off.
